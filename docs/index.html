      How my data is treated

      Your money is private. Here, in plain words, is where your information lives and what little can reach JP, who made this app.

      Where your data lives

      Everything you enter is saved in a Google Sheet that belongs to your own Google account, and your app runs in that same account. JP cannot open it, and neither can anyone else unless you share the sheet yourself. The app's files are public (on GitHub) and hold none of your data.

      What your app reads

      Your bank emails, only if you turned that on. Your app reads them (it can never send, change or delete an email) and turns each payment into an entry that waits for your OK. Those emails stay between your Gmail and your app.

      If you use the WhatsApp or Telegram bot, your Google Sheet keeps a copy of what each bot action changed for 30 days, so “undo” can put it back. Deleting an entry for good in Recently deleted, or Delete everything, removes it.

      Receipt photos and bank statement PDFs are read on your phone. The file is not uploaded and not kept.

      What can reach JP if you agree

      If you check “Help me improve the app”, your phone sends JP only small counts: how fast each action ran; which action failed, with a short code; whether each bank email was confirmed as it came, changed or discarded, and which field you changed (never what is in it); and how many times you open each screen. During setup, the installer page can also tell JP which step you reached, if you leave its box checked.

      What reaches JP only when you send it

      A sample of a bank email or statement that the app cannot read yet (you see the exact text first, and digits, names and email addresses are hidden before it leaves your phone), and the notes you write in “Send feedback to JP” (you see them first). Nothing else.

      Never sent to JP

      Your expenses, amounts, descriptions, categories you created, names of friends or payees, account names or numbers, your emails, your photos and PDFs, and your sign-in.
