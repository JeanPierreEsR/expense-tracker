Your accounts

Main banks

Other banks

Yape and Plin

Yape works only with BCP accounts. Plin works with accounts at other banks, not with BCP, and also with credit cards. Neither holds money of its own: each one runs on top of an account, so link the account it uses. You can change this later in More → Accounts, cards & platforms.

Heads up: don't add wallets that are tied to a bank account (for example, other banks' wallet apps). Add the bank account behind them instead. Only wallets that stand on their own, like Starbucks or other loyalty programs, are added separately, later, in More → Accounts, cards & platforms.
