Expense Tracker — setup test

Test page. Use it only with the spare Google account. It creates a new spreadsheet and backend in that account; your real app is not touched.

1. Create my tracker

Signs in with Google, then creates the spreadsheet and the backend in your account and publishes it.

One-time Google switch

Google asks you to turn on one setting before this page may create your tracker.

  1. Tap the button below — a Google page opens in a new tab.
  2. Turn on Google Apps Script API.
  3. Come back to this tab. It continues by itself (Google can take a few minutes to notice).
Open Google's switch

2. Give permission

Opens your new backend. Google asks you to approve it (it will say the app isn't verified — that's expected), then sends you back here.

Approved it but weren't sent back here?

3. Test the connection

Signs in to your backend like the app does, saves a test entry (S/ 1.00) and reads it back.

4. Push an update

Sends a newer copy of the backend, then checks it arrived and the test entry is still there.

Diagnostics

“Forget” only clears this page's memory. To remove the test from the account, delete the spreadsheet “Expense Tracker (setup test)” in Google Drive — its backend goes with it.
